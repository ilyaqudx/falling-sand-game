The Science: Cellular Automata & Fluid Dynamics

The Falling Sand Game is powered by a mathematical concept known as Cellular Automata (pioneered by John von Neumann and Stanislaw Ulam). The 2D world is divided into a discrete grid of cells, where each cell calculates its next state based on the states of its neighboring cells:

  • Stokes Law & Granular Friction: Sand particles check if the cell below is vacant; if blocked, they randomize diagonal checks to mimic natural friction and granular repose.
  • Navier-Stokes Fluid Approximation: Liquids sample horizontal dispersion distance, spreading outward until equilibrium pressure is attained.
  • Thermodynamic Phase Transitions: Water converts to steam at high temperatures; lava vitrifies into stone upon cooling; plants utilize water in simulated photosynthesis.
